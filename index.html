CORE ENGINEERING

Smart System Architecture

Four synchronized subsystems engineered to maximize ambient kinetic and solar recovery without compromising safety or handling.

☀️

Photovoltaic Roof

Ultra-thin flexible monocrystalline solar cells integrated into the curved carbon composite roof panel. Produces constant energy whether the vehicle is traveling or parked in sunlight.

23.4% CELL CONVERSION RATIO
🌀

Kinetic Tail Turbine

Duct-encased lightweight micro-wind turbine positioned at the low-pressure rear spoiler zone. Converts forced aerodynamic slipstream into rotational magnetic flux without drag.

NEODYMIUM PERMANENT MAGNET
🧠

Smart MPPT & BMS

Dual Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) microcontroller algorithms balance fluctuating solar voltages with turbulent wind generator inputs for safe charging.

AI-POWERED LOAD BALANCING
🔋

Dual-Stage Battery

Modular LiFePO4 battery pack protected by thermal heat spreaders and smart cell isolation. Capable of rapid trickle absorption and regenerative braking storage.
